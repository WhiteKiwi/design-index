# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Design Index is a collaborative learning resource. Be respectful, specific, and generous when discussing another person’s work or contribution.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable feedback about fit, evidence, and maintenance;
- distinguishing observation from preference;
- crediting original authors and canonical sources;
- accepting that curation decisions can change with better evidence.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misattribution, spam, and undisclosed promotion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `whitekiwi.dev@gmail.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific code of conduct informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
