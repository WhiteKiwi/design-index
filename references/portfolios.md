| Reference | Take | Leave |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Review questions

1. Can a first-time visitor say who this person is after one viewport?
2. Is the strongest evidence visible before the archive?
3. Does motion demonstrate craft or delay reading?
4. Are claims linked to work, writing, source, or external evidence?
5. Does mobile preserve identity and evidence instead of merely stacking desktop cards?
