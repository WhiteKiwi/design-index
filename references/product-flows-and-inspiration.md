# Product flows & inspiration

Use shipped-product references to study **behavior and sequence**. Use visual galleries to study **art direction and composition**. Neither replaces user research, accessibility review, or product evidence.

## Real product screens and flows

| Source | Best for | Study | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) | searchable screens, elements, and end-to-end mobile or web flows | how products solve the same job, where states appear, and which copy supports a decision | plan limits vary; popularity does not prove fit for another audience, market, or risk level |
| [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/) | screen recordings and annotated flows | timing, intermediate states, progressive disclosure, and complete task paths | recordings show observations, not business rules, research, or conversion impact |
| [Refero](https://refero.design/) | screens organized by pattern, product, and category | repeated information architecture and visual treatment | confirm recency and inspect the live product for behavior, accessibility, and current copy |
| [SaaSFrame](https://www.saasframe.io/) | SaaS marketing pages, product screens, and email flows | whether acquisition promises carry through onboarding, settings, and lifecycle messages | many screens and flows require Pro; captures do not establish current behavior or outcomes |

## Web art direction and composition

| Source | Best for | Study | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Recent (formerly Godly)](https://recent.design/) | expressive web references within a broader visual-design library | type scale, image treatment, transitions, composition, and one signature move | novelty-biased selection; validate clarity, load cost, motion comfort, keyboard use, and mobile behavior |
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/) | award-recognized experimental websites | one distinctive interaction or art-direction technique in its page context | jury scores and visual polish do not establish accessibility or product fit |
| [Siteinspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/) | websites filtered by style, type, subject, and platform | how a similar visual approach changes across content and sectors | gallery images omit interaction states, responsive behavior, and accessibility evidence |
| [Curated](https://curated.design/) | live sites grouped by industry and style | compare visual directions within one industry before selecting an approach | advanced filters require Pro; distinguish sponsored cards and templates from live references |
| [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/) | restrained typography-led web composition | hierarchy through spacing, alignment, scale, and quiet surfaces | minimal appearance can hide weak information scent, tiny controls, or insufficient contrast |

## Landing pages and sections

| Source | Best for | Study | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/) | one-page sites and section examples | pacing from proposition to evidence and action within a single page | its one-page constraint and template examples may not fit a complex product |
| [Landbook](https://land-book.com/) | website sections, motion, headlines, and social previews | how a page builds hierarchy beyond the hero and connects its sections | free browsing is limited; separate templates from shipped websites |
| [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/) | full-page landing captures and earlier versions | page-wide narrative, typography, and changes over time | screenshots and recordings may lag the live site and do not prove behavior |

## Interaction details

| Source | Best for | Study | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Design Spells](https://designspells.com/) | small interaction and motion details from real apps | trigger, feedback, timing, and recovery around one moment | delight can add latency or distraction; check the full task path and reduced-motion behavior |

## A better research method

1. Write the product job and the state you need to study.
2. Compare at least three shipped implementations, not three stylistic clones.
3. Capture sequence, content, edge cases, and input modality—not only color and layout.
4. Record what is observed separately from why you think it works.
5. Prototype with real constraints and verify with users or product data.

```text
flow library → observe behavior
visual gallery → explore art direction
design system → inspect rules
product research → decide
```
